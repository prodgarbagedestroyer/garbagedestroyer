## Overview

Compares three messaging patterns under identical workloads: persistent logs (Kafka), queue-based routing (RabbitMQ), and lightweight pub/sub (MQTT).

## Test Configuration

- **Brokers**: Kafka 3.9, RabbitMQ 4.0, Mosquitto 2.0
- **Workload**: 100K messages, 1KB payload, 3 consumers
- **Metrics**: throughput, end-to-end latency, broker CPU/memory

## Results (1KB messages, 3 consumers)

| Broker | Msg/s | p99 latency | Broker RSS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kafka | 850K | 2ms | 1.2GB |
| RabbitMQ | 120K | 8ms | 280MB |
| MQTT | 45K | 15ms | 45MB |
